You need a Solana wallet to pay

A wallet is what actually holds and sends the money — PayTag never touches your funds. It takes about a minute to set up:

  1. Install Phantom (browser extension on a computer, or the app on a phone).
  2. Open this link inside Phantom if you are on a phone — a normal mobile browser cannot see your wallet.
  3. Add a little SOL, or USDC if this link asks for it, then come back and tap "Connect Wallet to Pay".

Payments powered by Solana. Nothing is stored on a server.
